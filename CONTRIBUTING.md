# Contributing guidelines
Hi! 
At Ravena Innovation Station, we like it when contributors try to improve our robot code.
However, there are still rules. The reason this document exists is to make sure they are clear

## Legal notice
This document is specifically for contributors who ARE NOT affiliated with any team in competition with the Coeymans Chameleons. 
If you are affiliated with a competing team, you may not contribute because contributing may lead to a conflict of interest.

## Rules for contributors
1. No force pushing. Do so and you may be banned from contributing.
2. No backfilling commit history from another team's code repository.
3. You may not commit copyrighted or vibecoded content that you cannot legally grant the Chameleons the right to use into the repository
4. Do not submit files that are over 100 MB in size.
5. You may not create 2,147,483,647 branches in our Git repo (but why would you?)
6. Same goes with files. Do not create an excessive number of files just to get around the 100 MB file size limit. Keep it actually small.
7. Do not submit any offensive content, whether to defame the team or not.
8. Do not attempt to add rattlesnake-centric driving code (doing so may ruin the clean-room status of the codebase)
9. Do not truncate every file in the codebase
10. Do not use any AI to code our robot (you may use AI to troubleshoot developer tools or get quick answers to questions on your local machine, but you may not use it to generate code)
11. Do not submit any malware.
12. Do not submit any Chameleon S##### Abuse Material.
13. Do not play games when you are supposed to be coding
